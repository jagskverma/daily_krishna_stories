## Index entry
title: Krishna's Final Instruction to Uddhava
summary: Uddhava recalls how, as the Yadavas destroyed themselves, Krishna sat at the foot of an ashvattha tree and, finding Uddhava there, granted him the ancient teaching and the supreme state, commanding him to go to Badari.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 4 | verses 2-18 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'The Lord, seeing that course of his own maya, sipped the Sarasvati and sat at the foot of a tree; he said to me, go to Badari. Yet, unable to leave his feet, I followed; and he, finding me there, told me: I know your heart's desire; I grant you what is hard for others to attain. And he taught me the supreme state.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 2-10, 18)
