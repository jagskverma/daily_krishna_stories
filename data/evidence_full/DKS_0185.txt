## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 73 | verses 1-29 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The released kings, emaciated and haggard, gaze upon Krishna's dark four-armed form and bow at his feet in gratitude. Krishna assures them their devotion to him will be firm, bids them rule their realms righteously, has them bathed, feasted and adorned, and sends them home with chariots and kind words. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1-29)
