# STORY DKS_0447 — Krishna's Verdict on Ashvatthama

## Index entry
title: Krishna's Verdict on Ashvatthama
summary: When Ashvatthama, the murderer of the sleeping children, is dragged before Krishna and the Pandavas, Krishna urges that the brahmin-slayer must be punished but not slain, and pronounces the compromise Arjuna carries out: strip his jewel and shave his head, sparing his life.
