  excerpt: At the next parvan the terrible demon Balvala appears, raining filth upon the sacrificial hall. Balarama, remembering his plough and mace, drags the sky-roaming Balvala down with the plough's tip and crushes his skull with the mace, and the delighted sages honor Balarama and bestow blessings, a celestial garland and garments upon him.
