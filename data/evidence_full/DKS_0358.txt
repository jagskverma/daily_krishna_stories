## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 78 | verses 15-31 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Visiting the sages' sacrifice at Naimisha, Balarama sees the suta Romaharshana, a shudra by birth, seated among the brahmanas without rising to honor them. Enraged, Balarama strikes him down with a blade of kusha grass, and the sages cry out that he has committed the sin of slaying a brahmana, saying this was done without knowledge and must be expiated. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 15-31)
