summary: After one hundred and twenty-five years, Brahma and the assembled gods come to Dwaraka, praise Krishna, and remind him that the earth's burden is lifted and his clan is nearly consumed by the curse, asking him to return to his own abode.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 6 | verses 19-31 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Brahma said: 'A hundred and twenty-five years have passed since you descended in the Yadu line. Your work for the gods is all but done, and this clan is nearly destroyed by the brahmins' curse. If you think fit, return to your supreme abode.' Krishna answered that the earth's burden was indeed lifted and he would go at the end of the curse. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 24-30)
