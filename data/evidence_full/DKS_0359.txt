## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 78 | verses 32-39 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The sages tell Balarama that his atonement is to slay the cruel demon Balvala, son of Ilvala, who rains filth upon their sacrifice at every parvan, and then to wander Bharatavarsha bathing at the sacred fords for twelve months to be purified. Balarama accepts, undertaking the expiation for the world's benefit. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 32-39)
