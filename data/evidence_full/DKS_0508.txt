  excerpt: The avadhuta said: 'The python eats whatever comes by chance, whether fine or foul, much or little, and lies still for days without food if nothing comes. The sage is serene and unfathomable like the ocean, which neither swells when rivers pour in nor dries when they do not.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 1-5)
