## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 78 | verses 1-11 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Dantavakra, eager to repay his slain friends, rushes at Krishna on foot, taunting him as the murderer of his kinsmen, and strikes him on the head with his mace. Unshaken, Krishna crushes Dantavakra's chest with his mace Kaumodaki, and when his brother Viduratha comes on to avenge him, Krishna severs his crowned head with the discus. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1-11)
