title: The Elephant, the Honey-Gatherer, the Deer and the Fish
summary: The avadhuta learns from the elephant, ensnared by lust for the decoy she-elephant, from the honey-gatherer, whose hoard others enjoy, from the deer, snared while listening to music, and from the fish, hooked through the tongue, how the senses lead one to ruin.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 8 | verses 12-20 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The avadhuta said: 'The ascetic should not touch a woman even of wood, lest he be bound like the elephant caught by the she-elephant; the wealth painfully hoarded is enjoyed by others, as the honey-gatherer steals the bees' honey; the deer is snared by song; and the fish, allured by the tongue's bait, meets death on the hook.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 12-18)
