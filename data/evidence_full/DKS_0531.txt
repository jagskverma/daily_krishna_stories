# STORY DKS_0531 — Krishna's Last Message to Daruka

## Index entry
title: Krishna's Last Message to Daruka
summary: Krishna consoles the hunter, granting him heaven, and when his charioteer Daruka weeps at his feet, sends him to Dwaraka with the final message: the Yadavas are destroyed, Balarama is gone, and the sea will swallow Dwaraka, so let the people follow Arjuna to Indraprastha.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 30 | verses 38-49 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna said to Jara: 'Fear not; rise. This was my own doing. Go, with my leave, to the abode of the virtuous.' And to his charioteer he said: 'Go to Dwaraka and tell my kinsmen of the destruction and of Balarama's departure and of my state. The sea will soon flood the city I have left; let all of you, with Arjuna's help, go to Indraprastha.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 38-39, 45-47)
