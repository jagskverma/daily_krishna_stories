## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 16 | verses 53-67 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna bids Kaliya depart with his kin to the ocean, declaring that the mark of Krishna's feet will protect him from Garuda; Kaliya worships him and leaves, and the Yamuna becomes pure and sweet once more. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 53-67)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 2 | verses 30 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'The cows, perishing from drinking the poisoned water, he restored; subduing the king of serpents, he raised them up and made that water again fit to drink.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 30)
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 67 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: placing his feet on the hoods of the serpent-king that were above the water... he began to dance there... 'O serpent, I do not wish to allow you to live in this water of Yamuna. Therefore repair to the ocean with your wife and kinsmen.'
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 7 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Laying hold of the middle hood of their chief with both his hands, he bent it down, and set his foot upon the hitherto unbended head, and danced upon it in triumph... So saying, Hari set the snake-king at liberty, who... departed to the ocean.
