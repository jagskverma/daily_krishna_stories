## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 71 | verses 1-10 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava counsels that the Rajasuya is to be performed by one who has conquered all directions, so defeating Jarasandha serves both purposes. Since Jarasandha is the equal of Bhima alone and cannot be overthrown with an army, Bhima should go to him disguised as a brahmana, request single combat, and slay him before Krishna. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1-10)
