  excerpt: Pradyumna in a moment dispels the demon's illusions with divine weapons and showers arrows on Shalva's host, winning the admiration of both armies. But Dyuman, Shalva's minister, strikes Pradyumna on the chest with his mace, and the charioteer carries the senseless hero away; regaining consciousness, Pradyumna rebukes him, saying no Yadu may be seen fleeing the field.
