## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 8 | verses 7-15 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uttara cries: 'Protect me, protect me, great yogin, Lord of gods, master of the world! May this blazing iron-tipped arrow burn me, but let my embryo not fall.' The Lord, loving to his devotees, understood that Drona's son's weapon was aimed to leave the Pandavas without heir; the Lord, indwelling soul of all beings, covered the womb of Virata's daughter with his own maya to preserve the Kuru line.
