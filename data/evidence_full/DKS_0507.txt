# STORY DKS_0507 — The Pigeon and the Hunter's Net

## Index entry
title: The Pigeon and the Hunter's Net
summary: A pigeon so bound to his mate and chicks that when a hunter's net traps his family he casts himself into the same net and perishes with them, teaching Krishna's hearers that clinging to home and family destroys.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 7 | verses 51-73 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The pigeon, seeing his chicks and his mate caught in the hunter's net, lamented: 'Alas, my poor mind! My home is destroyed; of what use is my life now?' And he too flew into the net, and the cruel hunter took them all. 'Thus the householder who feeds his family like a bird, attached and without peace, comes to ruin.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 66-72)

53. kapotau sneha-guṇita- hṛdayau gṛha-dharmiṇau /
54. śayyāsanāṭana-sthāna vārtā-krīḍāśanādikam /
55. yaṃ yaṃ vāñchati sā rājan tarpayanty anukampitā /
56. kapotī prathamaṃ garbhaṃ gṛhṇantī kāla āgate /
57. teṣu kāle vyajāyanta racitāvayavā hareḥ /
58. prajāḥ pupuṣatuḥ prītau dampatī putra-vatsalau /
59. tāsāṃ patatraiḥ su-sparśaiḥ kūjitair mugdha-ceṣṭitaiḥ /
60. snehānubaddha-hṛdayāv anyonyaṃ viṣṇu-māyayā /
61. ekadā jagmatus tāsām annārthaṃ tau kuṭumbinau /
62. dṛṣṭvā tān lubdhakaḥ kaścid yadṛcchāto vane-caraḥ /
63. kapotaś ca kapotī ca prajā-poṣe sadotsukau /
64. kapotī svātmajān vīkṣya bālakān jāla-samvṛtān /
65. sāsakṛt sneha-guṇitā dīna-cittāja-māyayā /
66. kapotaḥ svātmajān baddhān ātmano 'py adhikān priyān /
67. aho me paśyatāpāyam alpa-puṇyasya durmateḥ /

69. so 'haṃ śūnye gṛhe dīno mṛta-dāro mṛta-prajaḥ /

71. taṃ labdhvā lubdhakaḥ krūraḥ kapotaṃ gṛha-medhinam /
72. evaṃ kuṭumby aśāntātmā dvandvārāmaḥ patatri-vat /
73. yaḥ prāpya mānuṣaṃ lokaṃ mukti-dvāram apāvṛtam /
