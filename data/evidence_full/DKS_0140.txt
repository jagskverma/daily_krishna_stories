## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 44 | verses 28-38 | confidence 5
  excerpt: As Kamsa ordered Nanda bound and Vasudeva killed, Krishna, enraged, leapt onto the high dais, seized Kamsa by the hair, hurled him into the arena and fell upon him, dragging his body about before all the people. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 28-38)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 2 | verses 34 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'Then, coming to the city of his parents with Balarama to free them, he hurled the leader of the enemy hosts from the high seat and dragged him lifeless upon the earth.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 34)
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 85 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: stretching his own Parigha-like arms Krishna drew Kansa by the hair in the arena... When... Kansa breathed his last Krishna threw away his body.
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 20 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: springing up to the place where he was seated, laid hold of him by the hair of his head, and struck his tiara to the ground: then casting him down upon the earth, Govinda threw himself upon him. Crushed by the weight of the upholder of the universe... Kansa the king, gave up the ghost.
