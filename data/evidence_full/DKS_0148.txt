## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 45 | verses 36-47 | confidence 5
  excerpt: As his teacher's fee, Sandipani asked for his son, who had been lost in the ocean at Prabhasa; Krishna slew the demon Panchajana, took his conch-shell body, and going to Yama's city, blew the conch and brought back the boy. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 36-46)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 3 | verses 1 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'Having studied the whole Veda once through from Sandipani, he granted his guru the boon of his dead son, bringing him back from the belly of Pancajana.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1)
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 88 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: The conch, which Janarddana obtained after slaying Panchajana, is known amongst gods and men as Panchajanya... having vanquished the fearful son of Vivaswan... obtained the child son of his preceptor.
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 21 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: having slain the vile Panchajana, he took the conch shell... The heroes also recovered the boy from the pains of death, and restored him in his former person to his father.
