### Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) 10.32 (1-14)
1. tāsām āvirabhūc chauriḥ smayamāna-mukhāmbujaḥ /
2. taṃ vilokyāgataṃ preṣṭhaṃ prīty-utphulla-dṛśo 'balāḥ /

6. aparānimiṣad-dṛgbhyāṃ juṣāṇā tan-mukhāmbujam /
7. taṃ kācin netra-randhreṇa hṛdi kṛtvā nimīlya ca /
8. sarvās tāḥ keśavāloka- paramotsava-nirvṛtāḥ /
9. tābhir vidhūta-śokābhir bhagavān acyuto vṛtaḥ /
10. tāḥ samādāya kālindyā nirviśya pulinaṃ vibhuḥ /
11. śarac-candrāṃśu-sandoha- dhvasta-doṣā-tamaḥ śivam /
12. tad-darśanāhlāda-vidhūta-hṛd-rujo manorathāntaṃ śrutayo yathā yayuḥ /
13. tatropaviṣṭo bhagavān sa īśvaro yogeśvarāntar-hṛdi kalpitāsanaḥ /
