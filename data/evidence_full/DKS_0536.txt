## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 88 | verses 28-38 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Vishnu, taking the form of a radiant young brahmachari, receives the exhausted Vrikasura and, feigning disbelief in Shiva's boon, tells him to test it by placing his hand on his own head. The bewildered asura does so and instantly splits his own skull and dies, while the gods shower flowers and the freed Shiva praises Vishnu, the deliverer of those who offend the great.
