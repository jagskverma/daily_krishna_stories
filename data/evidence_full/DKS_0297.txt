## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 89 | verses 28-40 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Arjuna, hearing the brahmana's lament, vows that he will protect the next child or enter fire, and when the time comes he rings the delivery-room with a cage of arrows. But the newborn vanishes bodily into the sky, and the brahmana mocks Arjuna, saying that even Balarama, Krishna, Pradyumna and Aniruddha could not save the child, and curses Arjuna's boasting. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 28-40)
