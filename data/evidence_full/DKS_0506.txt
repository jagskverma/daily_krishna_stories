## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 7 | verses 36-50 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The avadhuta said: 'Though trampled by all beings, the wise one does not stray from his path — this I learned from the earth. Moving among the objects of sense, the yogi remains unattached like the wind; pure and sweet like water; untainted by what he consumes, like fire; waxing and waning like the moon; one soul appearing in many bodies, like the sun.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 36-50)

38. prāṇa-vṛttyaiva santuṣyen munir naivendriya-priyaiḥ /
39. viṣayeṣv āviśan yogī nānā-dharmeṣu sarvataḥ /
40. pārthiveṣv iha deheṣu praviṣṭas tad-guṇāśrayaḥ /

42. tejo-'b-anna-mayair bhāvair meghādyair vāyuneritaiḥ /
43. svacchaḥ prakṛtitaḥ snigdho mādhuryas tīrtha-bhūr nṛṇām /
44. tejasvī tapasā dīpto durdharṣodara-bhājanaḥ /
45. kvacic channaḥ kvacit spaṣṭa upāsyaḥ śreya icchatām /
46. sva-māyayā sṛṣṭam idaṃ sad-asal-lakṣaṇaṃ vibhuḥ /
47. visargādyāḥ śmaśānāntā bhāvā dehasya nātmanaḥ /
48. kālena hy ogha-vegena bhūtānāṃ prabhavāpyayau /
49. guṇair guṇān upādatte yathā-kālaṃ vimuñcati /
50. budhyate sve na bhedena vyakti-stha iva tad-gataḥ /
