## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 84 | verses 58-69 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Vasudeva, weeping as he recalls the friendship, tells Nanda that the snare called affection is hard to cast off, and that blinded by royal splendor he neglected his old friend. Nanda stays three months, honored by the Yadavas, and then, laden with rich gifts from Vasudeva, Ugrasena, Krishna and Uddhava, departs with the cowherds, his heart fixed on Krishna's lotus feet.
