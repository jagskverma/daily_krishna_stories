## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 58 | verses 55-57 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna wed Bhadra, daughter of his aunt Srutakirti, given by her brothers; and he carried off Lakshmana, the fair daughter of the king of Madra, from her svayamvara like Garuda seizing nectar. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 55-57)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 83 | verses 16-38 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Asked by Draupadi how Krishna married them, Lakshmana recounts that her father arranged a svayamvara with a fish target, at which kings from everywhere failed even to string the bow. Krishna took up the bow, strung it effortlessly, and with a single arrow shot the fish, and when the envious kings attacked he cut them down with his arrows and carried Lakshmana to Dwaraka. (rendered from Sanskrit, v
