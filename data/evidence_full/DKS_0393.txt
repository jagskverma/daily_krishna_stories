## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 70 | verses 31-46 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The divine sage Narada, radiant with matted tawny locks, appears in the assembly and is honored by Krishna. He announces that Krishna's cousin Yudhishthira, desiring imperial sovereignty, will worship Krishna with the Rajasuya sacrifice, and that Krishna should consent. Krishna then playfully asks Uddhava for his counsel. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 31-46)
