## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 82 | verses 1-15 | confidence 5
  excerpt: A great solar eclipse occurs, and the Yadavas, together with Vasudeva, Akrura and others, journey to Samanta-panchaka, the field where Rama had once made lakes of the kings' blood, to bathe and give cows and gifts. There they meet the kings of many realms and their own long-separated kin, and embrace one another with tears of joy. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1-15)
