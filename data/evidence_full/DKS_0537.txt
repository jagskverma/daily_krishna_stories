  excerpt: To settle which of the three gods is greatest, the sages send Bhrigu; Brahma grows angry at Bhrigu's disrespect but restrains himself, and Shiva raises his trident to slay him till the goddess intervenes. Vishnu, however, on being kicked in the chest by Bhrigu, rises and apologizes, asking the sage to forgive him — and Bhrigu, moved, declares Vishnu alone the abode of peace and fearlessness. (rend
