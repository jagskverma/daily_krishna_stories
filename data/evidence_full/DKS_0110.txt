## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 47 | verses 21-51 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna's message: 'My separation from you is never complete, as ether, air, fire, water and earth pervade all beings... fix your mind wholly on me and you will soon attain me.' The gopis reply that every river, hill and forest reminds them of Krishna and they cannot forget him. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 21-51)
