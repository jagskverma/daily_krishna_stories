## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 9 | verses 13-22 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The avadhuta said: 'Building a house brings only sorrow — the serpent enters another's abandoned home and thrives. As the spider sends forth the thread from its own heart, plays with it and draws it back, so the Lord creates, sustains and withdraws the world. And as the wasp, meditating on its captor, becomes like it, so the mind becomes what it dwells upon.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 14, 20
