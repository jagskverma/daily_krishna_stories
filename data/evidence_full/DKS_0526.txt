# STORY DKS_0526 — Arjuna Brings News of the Yadava End

## Index entry
title: Arjuna Brings News of the Yadava End
summary: Arjuna returns from Dwaraka broken and weeping and tells Yudhishthira how the Yadavas, deluded by a brahmin's curse and drunk on wine, slew one another with their fists, and how Krishna then departed the earth, after which the Kali age set in.
life_stage: later_life | story_arc: krishna_departure

locations: Dwaraka, Hastinapura
