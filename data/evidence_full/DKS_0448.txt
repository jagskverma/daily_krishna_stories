## Index entry
title: Bhishma's Passing in Krishna's Presence
summary: Lying on his bed of arrows at the auspicious northern solstice, Bhishma fixes his gaze on Krishna standing before him, glorifies him as Narayana himself, and passes away with his mind merged in the Lord, while the sages and Pandavas look on.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 9 | verses 21-45 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Bhishma says: 'This very Krishna, who came to me as I give up my life, out of love for his unalloyed devotees — may he be my refuge.' Then, gathering in his speech, he fixed his unblinking gaze upon the four-armed Krishna standing before him in yellow robes, and offered his final praise: 'May my love rest on Krishna...' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 21, 29-31)
