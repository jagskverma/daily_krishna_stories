# STORY DKS_0519 — The Sages Curse Samba and the Yadavas

## Index entry
title: The Sages Curse Samba and the Yadavas
summary: Yadu boys dress Samba as a pregnant woman and ask the visiting sages what 'she' will bear; the angered sages curse that he will bring forth an iron mace to destroy the clan, and the mace appears, is ground and cast into the sea, and its remaining iron, swallowed by a fish, later becomes the hunter's arrow.
life_stage: dwaraka | story_arc: end_of_yadavas
characters: Krishna, Samba, the Yadu boys, the sages, Ugrasena
locations: Dwaraka, the seashore
themes: curse, fate, arrogance, the iron mace
festival_tags: 
age_sensitivity: all | notes: Same curse episode (BP 11.1, MB 16.1): youths disguise Samba as a pregnant woman; the sages curse him to bear the iron bolt.
