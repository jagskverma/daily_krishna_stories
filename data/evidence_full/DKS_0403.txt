- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 77 | verses 5-18 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna, summoned to Indraprastha for the Rajasuya, returns to Dwaraka perceiving dire omens, and realizing the Chaidya's allies have attacked his city, orders Daruka to drive his chariot against Shalva. Shalva hurls a blazing spear at Krishna's charioteer, which Krishna cuts to a hundred pieces with his arrows, and he pierces Shalva with sixteen shafts. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 5-18)
