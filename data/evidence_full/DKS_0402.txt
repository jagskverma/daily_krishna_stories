## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 76 | verses 7-16 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Obtaining the self-moving, impenetrable vehicle Saubha, Shalva flies to Dwaraka remembering the enmity of the Vrishnis, and with a vast army shatters its gardens and gates, raining weapons, rocks, trees and serpents upon the city. Pradyumna, seeing his people afflicted, mounts his chariot and cries 'Do not fear,' and the Yadava warriors sally forth. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 7-16)
