## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 80 | verses 5-16 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Sudama, a learned and self-controlled brahmana living in great poverty, is urged by his devoted wife to visit Krishna, his boyhood friend who now dwells in Dwaraka, saying Krishna will give them wealth. She borrows four handfuls of flattened rice and ties them in a rag as a gift, and Sudama sets out for Dwaraka wondering how he may gain sight of Krishna. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 5-16)
