## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 86 | verses 23-36 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna travels to Mithila with the sages, and both the brahmana Shrutadeva and King Bahulashva, each thinking Krishna has come to his own house, invite him and the sages. To please both devotees, Krishna enters both homes at once, unseen by either, and each worships him with overflowing devotion. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 23-36)
