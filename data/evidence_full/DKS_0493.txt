## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 82 | verses 38-47 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The gopis, seeing Krishna after so long, embrace him in their hearts and attain that state which even the ever-yoked cannot easily gain. Krishna asks whether they remember his friendship, and teaches them that as the wind scatters clouds, so the Lord unites and separates beings, and that devotion to him grants immortality — by which instruction the gopis, their bondage dissolved, attained him.
