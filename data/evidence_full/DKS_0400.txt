  excerpt: In the wondrous hall built by Maya, Duryodhana mistakes a crystal floor for water and lifts his garment, then falls where he thought the floor was, deceived by the illusion. Bhima and the women laugh despite the king's restraint, and Duryodhana, humiliated and blazing with rage, departs in silence, while Krishna remains silent, intent on relieving the earth's burden.
