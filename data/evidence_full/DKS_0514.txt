# STORY DKS_0514 — The Swan Teaches the Kumaras

## Index entry
title: The Swan Teaches the Kumaras
summary: Asked by his disciples about the swan form, Krishna recounts how Brahma's sons Sanaka and the others, unable to get an answer from Brahma, were taught by Krishna himself appearing as a swan (hamsa), who resolved their doubt.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 11 | chapter/section 13 | verses 15-41 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna said: 'The mind-born sons of Brahma asked him the subtle goal of yoga; pondering their question, Brahma meditated on me, and I appeared to them in the form of a swan. They asked who I was, and I answered that if in truth there is no difference in the self, their very question has no ground — know that I alone am, and nothing apart from me.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 15-23)
