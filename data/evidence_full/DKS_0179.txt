## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 71 | verses 11-21 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna orders the departure, entrusts Dwaraka to Balarama, and mounts his Garuda-bannered chariot. Surrounded by an immense army with resounding drums, conches and trumpets, and followed by his queens in their conveyances, he sets out, crossing many lands, rivers and towns on the way to Indraprastha. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 11-21)
