summary: On the banks of the Yamuna, Vidura meets Uddhava, who is plunged in grief and remembrance of Krishna's departure, and hears him lament that the Yadavas never recognized the Lord who dwelt among them.

  excerpt: Uddhava, asked by Vidura, was for a moment silent, sunk in the nectar of Krishna's feet, his limbs thrilling with devotion; then, wiping his eyes, he smiled and said: 'The sun Krishna has set; what good news can I tell in homes bereft of fortune? Wretched is this world, and more so the Yadavas, who dwelt with Hari and knew him not.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 3-7)
