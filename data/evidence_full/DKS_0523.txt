# STORY DKS_0523 — The Yadava Fratricide at Prabhasa

## Index entry
title: The Yadava Fratricide at Prabhasa
summary: Drunk on maireya wine at Prabhasa, the Yadavas, deluded by Krishna's maya and the brahmins' curse, fall upon one another, and when their weapons fail they seize the eraka reeds — which have become iron clubs — and annihilate one another; Balarama then yields up his body in yoga.
life_stage: later_life | story_arc: end_of_yadavas
characters: Krishna, Balarama, the Yadavas
locations: Prabhasa
themes: destruction, drunkenness, the curse, fratricide
festival_tags: 
age_sensitivity: mild_violence | notes: Same catastrophe (VP 5.37, BP 11.30, MB 16.3): the kinsmen destroy one another with reed-weapons.

- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 4 | verses 1 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'Their minds deranged by the fault of the maireya wine, at sunset there was among them a crushing of one another, like reeds.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 1)
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 37 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: As they drank, the destructive flame of dissension was kindled amongst them... The rushes in their hands became like thunderbolts, and they struck one another with them fatal blows... In a short time there was not a single Yádava left alive, except the mighty Krishńa and Dáruka.
- Mahabharata (Ganguli translation) | book/canto 16 | chapter/section 3 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Then Yuyudhana, inebriated with wine, derisively laughing at and insulting Kritavarma in the midst of that assembly, said, 'What Kshatriya is there who... will slay men locked in the embraces of sleep and, therefore, already dead?' ... Having said these words, Satyaki rushed at Kritavarma and severed his head with a sword in the very sight of Keshava.
- Mahabharata (Ganguli translation) | book/canto 16 | chapter/section 3 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: The delighter of the Yadus, beholding his own son, and the son of Sini too, slain, took up, in wrath, a handful of the Eraka grass that grew there. That handful of grass became a terrible bolt of iron endued with the energy of the thunderbolt. With it Krishna slew all those that came before him... Son killed sire, and sire killed son, O Bharata.
