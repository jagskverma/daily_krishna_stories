# STORY DKS_0265 — Krishna Uproots the Parijata Tree from Indra's Heaven

## Index entry
title: Krishna Uproots the Parijata Tree from Indra's Heaven
summary: Krishna returns Aditi's earrings to the gods, and at Satyabhama's wish uproots the Parijata tree from Indra's heaven, defeating the gods.
life_stage: dwaraka | story_arc: narakasura_and_gods
characters: Krishna, Satyabhama, Indra, Aditi
locations: Indra's heaven, Dvaraka
themes: desire, triumph, divine gift
festival_tags: 
age_sensitivity: all | notes: VP splits earrings-restoration (vp5_030_01) from the uprooting (vp5_030_02).

## Canonical event
event_id: evt_0212
variant_notes: BP 10.59 and Harivamsha 1.121 bundle the restoration of Aditi's earrings with the uprooting; bpc1_041 is Uddhava's recall.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 59 | verses 37-40 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna returned the earrings to Aditi and was worshipped by Indra; urged by Satyabhama, he uprooted the Parijata tree, overcame Indra and the gods, and brought it to Dvaraka, planting it in Satyabhama's garden. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 37-40)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 3 | verses 4 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'Wishing, like a common man, to please his beloved, the Lord went for the celestial tree; the thunderbolt-wielder, blind with rage, rushed at him with his host, as if he were a plaything of the women.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 4)
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 121 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Giving to the king of gods the pair of ear-rings belonging to Aditi and saluting him ... Aditi was pleased and took up her two sons on her lap. ... Krishna of unlimited powers, forcibly up-rooted it [the Pārijata] and placed it on Garuda's head.
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 220 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: the illustrious Adhokshaja saw hosts of celestial warriors... That refuge of the pious, the mighty Krishna uprooted the Pārijāta tree and placed it on the back of Garuda even before their eyes
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 30 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Hari smiled upon her, and taking the Párijáta plant, put it upon Garud́a... 'This Párijáta tree belongs to Śachí... it is not proper, Govinda, for you to remove it.'
