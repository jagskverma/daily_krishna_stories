## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 90 | verses 28-45 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Krishna has sixteen thousand and more than a hundred queens, and on each he begets ten sons; among the foremost are Pradyumna, Aniruddha, Samba and others. Pradyumna's grandson through Aniruddha is Vajra, who alone would survive the destruction by the mace, and the Yadava line is declared too vast to count. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 28-45)
