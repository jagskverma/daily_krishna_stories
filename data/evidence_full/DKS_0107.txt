## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 33 | verses 1-24 | confidence 5
  excerpt: There Govinda began the rasa dance with the gopis, multiplying himself to stand between every pair; the gods rained flowers, the celestial women watching were bewildered, and Krishna sported with them through the moonlit autumn nights. (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 1-24)
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 3 | chapter/section 2 | verses 33 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Uddhava said: 'Honouring the face of night washed by the rays of the autumn moon, he sang sweet songs and sported as the ornament of the circle of the women.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 33)
- Harivamsha (Dutt, Gutenberg #61937) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 75 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Thus encircled by the milk-women Krishna used to sport, of his own accord, in the autumnal night adorned by the moon.
- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 13 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: Krishńa thought the lovely moonlight night of autumn propitious to the Rasa dance... Then proceeded the dance to the music of their clashing bracelets, and songs that celebrated in suitable strain the charms of the autumnal season.
