## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 88 | verses 12-27 | confidence 5
  excerpt: The asura Vrikasura, son of Shakuni, performs terrible penance and wins from the compassionate Shiva the boon that whomever he touches on the head shall die. To test it, he tries to place his hand on Shiva's own head, and the terrified Shiva, pursued by the asura, flees to the ends of the earth and heavens and finally takes refuge in Vaikuntha. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 12-27)
