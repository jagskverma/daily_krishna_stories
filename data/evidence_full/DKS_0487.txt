title: Kunti's Farewell Hymn to Krishna
summary: As Krishna prepares to leave Hastinapura for Dwaraka, Queen Kunti offers a parting eulogy recalling how he saved her family from poison, fire, exile and weapons, and asks only that his remembrance ever bind her; Krishna smiles and grants her prayer.

  excerpt: Freed from the brahma weapon, Kunti addressed Krishna as he turned to depart: 'I bow to you, the primal Person... You freed Devaki and me, with my sons, again and again from danger — from poison, from the great fire, from the cruel assembly, from the hardships of forest exile, from the weapons of great chariot-warriors.'
