## Index entry
title: Krishna Guides Arjuna Against Ashvatthama's Brahmastra
summary: After Ashvatthama releases the Brahmashira weapon at the Pandavas, a terrified Arjuna calls on Krishna, who tells him only a brahmastra can counter it; Arjuna discharges it and, at Krishna's word, withdraws both blazing weapons to save the worlds.

## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 1 | chapter/section 7 | verses 12-31 | confidence 5
  excerpt: When the blazing weapon flares in every direction, Arjuna cries out, 'Krishna, Krishna, mighty-armed one, giver of fearlessness to your devotees, you alone are the rescue of those being burned.' Krishna answers: 'Know this to be the brahma weapon of Drona's son. He does not know how to recall it. Only an astra can quell an astra.' (rendered from Sanskrit, verses 21, 26-27)
