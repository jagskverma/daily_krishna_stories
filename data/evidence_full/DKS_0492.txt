  excerpt: Nanda arrives with the cowherds to see Krishna, and the Vrishnis embrace him as if life itself had returned. Krishna and Balarama bow to their parents and, speechless with love, embrace them; Yashoda takes her two sons on her lap, and Devaki and Rohini, remembering the friendship of old, embrace Yashoda and speak of the love shown to the boys in Gokula.
