## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 85 | verses 33-51 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Entering Sutala by their yoga-maya, Krishna and Balarama are received by the daitya king Bali, who rises, bows, and washes their feet with his kin. Krishna then reveals that the six sons were once the six sons of Marichi, who laughed at Brahma and were cursed to be born as demons — the very children Kamsa killed, who now dwell nearby awaiting release. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 33-51)
