## Sources and evidence excerpts
- Srimad Bhagavata Purana (Sanskrit, GRETIL) | book/canto 10 | chapter/section 80 | verses 16-26 | confidence 5
  excerpt: Seeing Sudama from afar, Krishna rises from Rukmini's couch, runs to him, and embraces his old friend with tears of joy, then seats him on his own couch, washes his feet and bears the water on his head. The goddess Rukmini herself fans the emaciated brahmana, and the women of the palace marvel at the honor shown to a poor mendicant. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 16-26)
