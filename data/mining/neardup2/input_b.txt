DKS_0533 | Srimad Bhagavata Purana (San | 11.31 | Krishna's Final Departure / Ascension || As Brahma, Shiva and the hosts of gods watch, Krishna, seeing Brahma before him, closes his lotus eyes, draws his opulences into himself, consumes his body with the fire of yoga, and enters his own abode, his path unfathomable even to the gods.
DKS_0534 | Srimad Bhagavata Purana (San | 11.31 | The Sea Swallows Dwaraka || After Krishna's departure, his queens and family ascend the funeral pyres, Arjuna performs the last rites, and the sea floods and swallows Dwaraka — all but the Lord's own dwelling; Arjuna leads the survivors to Indraprastha and installs Vajra.
DKS_0535 | Mahabharata (Ganguli transla | 16.4 | Krishna Entrusts the Women to Vasudeva || Krishna returns to Dwaraka, tells his father Vasudeva to protect the women until Arjuna comes, touches his father's feet, and retraces his steps to comfort the wailing ladies.
DKS_0536 | Srimad Bhagavata Purana (San | 10.88 | Vrikasura's Lethal Boon from Shiva || The asura Vrikasura wins from Shiva the boon that whoever's head he touches will die, then tries to test it on Shiva himself, who flees in terror to Vaikuntha.
DKS_0537 | Srimad Bhagavata Purana (San | 10.88 | Vishnu Saves Shiva from Vrikasura || Vishnu, appearing as a young brahmachari, tricks Vrikasura into touching his own head, and the asura destroys himself, freeing Shiva.
DKS_0538 | Srimad Bhagavata Purana (San | 10.89 | Bhrigu Tests the Three Gods || Sent by the sages to test who is greatest, Bhrigu finds Brahma and Shiva angered, but Vishnu, far from being offended, apologizes and serves him, proving Vishnu supreme.
