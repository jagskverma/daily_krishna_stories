  excerpt: Taking the baby to her breast, the demoness is seized in her vital parts; Krishna drinks out her life along with her milk, and with a terrible cry she dies and crashes down in her monstrous true form. (rendered from Sanskrit, verse 6-12)

- Vishnu Purana (Wilson 1840) | book/canto 5 | chapter/section 5 | verses  | confidence 4
  excerpt: the female fiend Pútaná, the child-killer, came thither by night, and finding the little Krishńa asleep, took him up, and gave him her breast to suck... but Krishńa... sucked it with such violence, that he drained it of the life.
