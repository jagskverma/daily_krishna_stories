Future kings of Magadhá: descendants of Vrihadratha.

I WILL now relate to you the descendants of Vrihadratha, who will be the kings of Magadhá. There have been several powerful princes of this dynasty, of whom the most celebrated was Jarásandha; his son was Sahadeva; his son is Somápi his son will be Śrutavat his son will be Ayutáyus his son will be Niramitra his son will be Sukshatra his son will be Vrihatkarman his son will be Senajit his son will be Śrutanjaya his son will be Vipra his son will be Śuchi his son will be Kshemya his son will be Suvrata his son will be Dharma his son will be Suśuma his son will be Drid́hasena his son will be Sumati his son will be Suvala his son will be Suníta his son will be Satyajit his son will be Viśwajit his son will be Ripunjaya. These are the Várhadrathas, who will reign for a thousand years.
