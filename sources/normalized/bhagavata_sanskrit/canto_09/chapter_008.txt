1. bharukastatsutastasmādvṛkastasyāpi bāhukaḥ /
so 'ribhirhṛtabhū rājā sabhāryo vanamāviśat
2. vṛddhaṃ taṃ pañcatāṃ prāptaṃ mahiṣyanumariṣyatī /
aurveṇa jānatātmānaṃ prajāvantaṃ nivāritā
3. ājñāyāsyai sapatnībhirgaro datto 'ndhasā saha /
saha tenaiva sañjātaḥ sagarākhyo mahāyaśāḥ
4. sagaraścakravartyāsīt sāgaro yatsutaiḥ kṛtaḥ /
yastālajaṅghān yavanāñ chakān haihayabarbarān
5. nāvadhīdguruvākyena cakre vikṛtaveṣiṇaḥ /
muṇḍān chmaśrudharān kāṃścin muktakeśārdhamuṇḍitān
6. anantarvāsasaḥ kāṃścidabahirvāsaso 'parān /
so 'śvamedhairayajata sarvavedasurātmakam
7. aurvopadiṣṭayogena harimātmānamīśvaram /
tasyotsṛṣṭaṃ paśuṃ yajñe jahārāśvaṃ purandaraḥ
8. sumatyāstanayā dṛptāḥ piturādeśakāriṇaḥ /
hayamanveṣamāṇāste samantān nyakhanan mahīm
9. prāgudīcyāṃ diśi hayaṃ dadṛśuḥ kapilāntike /
eṣa vājiharaścaura āste mīlitalocanaḥ
10. hanyatāṃ hanyatāṃ pāpa iti ṣaṣṭisahasriṇaḥ /
udāyudhā abhiyayurunmimeṣa tadā muniḥ
11. svaśarīrāgninā tāvan mahendrahṛtacetasaḥ /
mahadvyatikramahatā bhasmasādabhavan kṣaṇāt
12. na sādhuvādo munikopabharjitā nṛpendraputrā iti sattvadhāmani /
kathaṃ tamo roṣamayaṃ vibhāvyate jagatpavitrātmani khe rajo bhuvaḥ
13. yasyeritā sāṅkhyamayī dṛḍheha naur yayā mumukṣustarate duratyayam /
bhavārṇavaṃ mṛtyupathaṃ vipaścitaḥ parātmabhūtasya kathaṃ pṛthaṅmatiḥ
14. yo 'samañjasa ityuktaḥ sa keśinyā nṛpātmajaḥ /
tasya putro 'ṃśumān nāma pitāmahahite rataḥ
15. asamañjasa ātmānaṃ darśayannasamañjasam /
jātismaraḥ purā saṅgādyogī yogādvicālitaḥ
16. ācaran garhitaṃ loke jñātīnāṃ karma vipriyam /
sarayvāṃ krīḍato bālān prāsyadudvejayan janam
17. evaṃ vṛttaḥ parityaktaḥ pitrā snehamapohya vai /
yogaiśvaryeṇa bālāṃstān darśayitvā tato yayau
18. ayodhyāvāsinaḥ sarve bālakān punarāgatān /
dṛṣṭvā visismire rājan rājā cāpyanvatapyata
19. aṃśumāṃścodito rājñā turagānveṣaṇe yayau /
pitṛvyakhātānupathaṃ bhasmānti dadṛśe hayam
20. tatrāsīnaṃ muniṃ vīkṣya kapilākhyamadhokṣajam /
astaut samāhitamanāḥ prāñjaliḥ praṇato mahān
21. bhp_09.08.022/0 aṃśumān uvāca 
na paśyati tvāṃ paramātmano 'jano na budhyate 'dyāpi samādhiyuktibhiḥ /
kuto 'pare tasya manaḥśarīradhī visargasṛṣṭā vayamaprakāśāḥ
22. ye dehabhājastriguṇapradhānā guṇān vipaśyantyuta vā tamaśca /
yanmāyayā mohitacetasastvāṃ viduḥ svasaṃsthaṃ na bahiḥprakāśāḥ
23. taṃ tvāṃ ahaṃ jñānaghanaṃ svabhāva pradhvastamāyāguṇabhedamohaiḥ /
sanandanādyairmunibhirvibhāvyaṃ kathaṃ vimūḍhaḥ paribhāvayāmi
24. praśānta māyāguṇakarmaliṅgam anāmarūpaṃ sadasadvimuktam /
jñānopadeśāya gṛhītadehaṃ namāmahe tvāṃ puruṣaṃ purāṇam
25. tvanmāyāracite loke vastubuddhyā gṛhādiṣu /
bhramanti kāmalobherṣyā mohavibhrāntacetasaḥ
26. adya naḥ sarvabhūtātman kāmakarmendriyāśayaḥ /
mohapāśo dṛḍhaśchinno bhagavaṃstava darśanāt
27. bhp_09.08.028/0 śrīśuka uvāca

aṃśumantamuvācedamanugrāhya dhiyā nṛpa
28. bhp_09.08.029/0 śrībhagavān uvāca

ime ca pitaro dagdhā gaṅgāmbho 'rhanti netarat
29. taṃ parikramya śirasā prasādya hayamānayat /
sagarastena paśunā yajñaśeṣaṃ samāpayat
30. rājyamaṃśumate nyasya niḥspṛho muktabandhanaḥ /
aurvopadiṣṭamārgeṇa lebhe gatimanuttamām
