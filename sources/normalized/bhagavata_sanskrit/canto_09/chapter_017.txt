1. anenā iti rājendra śṛṇu kṣatravṛdho 'nvayam /
kṣatravṛddhasutasyāsan suhotrasyātmajāstrayaḥ
2. kāśyaḥ kuśo gṛtsamada iti gṛtsamadādabhūt /
śunakaḥ śaunako yasya bahvṛcapravaro muniḥ
3. kāśyasya kāśistatputro rāṣṭro dīrghatamaḥpitā /
dhanvantarirdīrghatamasa āyurvedapravartakaḥ
4. yajñabhug vāsudevāṃśaḥ smṛtamātrārtināśanaḥ /
tatputraḥ ketumān asya jajñe bhīmarathastataḥ
5. divodāso dyumāṃstasmāt pratardana iti smṛtaḥ /
sa eva śatrujidvatsa ṛtadhvaja itīritaḥ /
tathā kuvalayāśveti prokto 'larkādayastataḥ
6. ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣasahasrāṇi ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣaśatāni ca /
nālarkādaparo rājan bubhuje medinīṃ yuvā
7. alarkāt santatistasmāt sunītho 'tha niketanaḥ /
dharmaketuḥ sutastasmāt satyaketurajāyata
8. dhṛṣṭaketustatastasmāt sukumāraḥ kṣitīśvaraḥ /
vītihotro 'sya bhargo 'to bhārgabhūmirabhūn nṛpa
9. itīme kāśayo bhūpāḥ kṣatravṛddhānvayāyinaḥ /
rābhasya rabhasaḥ putro gambhīraścākriyastataḥ
10. tadgotraṃ brahmavij jajñe śṛṇu vaṃśamanenasaḥ /
śuddhastataḥ śucistasmāc citrakṛddharmasārathiḥ
11. tataḥ śāntarajo jajñe kṛtakṛtyaḥ sa ātmavān /
rajeḥ pañcaśatānyāsan putrāṇāmamitaujasām
12. devairabhyarthito daityān hatvendrāyādadāddivam /
indrastasmai punardattvā gṛhītvā caraṇau rajeḥ
13. ātmānamarpayāmāsa prahrādādyariśaṅkitaḥ /
pitaryuparate putrā yācamānāya no daduḥ
14. triviṣṭapaṃ mahendrāya yajñabhāgān samādaduḥ /
guruṇā hūyamāne 'gnau balabhit tanayān rajeḥ
15. avadhīdbhraṃśitān mārgān na kaścidavaśeṣitaḥ /
kuśāt pratiḥ kṣātravṛddhāt sañjayastatsuto jayaḥ
16. tataḥ kṛtaḥ kṛtasyāpi jajñe haryabalo nṛpaḥ /
sahadevastato hīno jayasenastu tatsutaḥ
17. saṅkṛtistasya ca jayaḥ kṣatradharmā mahārathaḥ /
kṣatravṛddhānvayā bhūpā ime śṛṇv atha nāhuṣān
