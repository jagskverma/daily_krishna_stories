1. śṛṇu bhārgavyamūṃ gāthāṃ madvidhācaritāṃ bhuvi /
dhīrā yasyānuśocanti vane grāmanivāsinaḥ
2. basta eko vane kaścidvicinvan priyamātmanaḥ /
dadarśa kūpe patitāṃ svakarmavaśagāmajām
3. tasyā uddharaṇopāyaṃ bastaḥ kāmī vicintayan /
vyadhatta tīrthamuddhṛtya viṣāṇāgreṇa rodhasī
4. sottīrya kūpāt suśroṇī tameva cakame kila /
tayā vṛtaṃ samudvīkṣya bahvyo 'jāḥ kāntakāminīḥ
5. pīvānaṃ śmaśrulaṃ preṣṭhaṃ mīḍhvāṃsaṃ yābhakovidam /
sa eko 'javṛṣastāsāṃ bahvīnāṃ rativardhanaḥ /
reme kāmagrahagrasta ātmānaṃ nāvabudhyata
6. tameva preṣṭhatamayā ramamāṇamajānyayā /
vilokya kūpasaṃvignā nāmṛṣyadbastakarma tat
7. taṃ durhṛdaṃ suhṛdrūpaṃ kāminaṃ kṣaṇasauhṛdam /
indriyārāmamutsṛjya svāminaṃ duḥkhitā yayau
8. so 'pi cānugataḥ straiṇaḥ kṛpaṇastāṃ prasāditum /
kurvanniḍaviḍākāraṃ nāśaknot pathi sandhitum
9. tasya tatra dvijaḥ kaścidajāsvāmyacchinadruṣā /
lambantaṃ vṛṣaṇaṃ bhūyaḥ sandadhe 'rthāya yogavit
10. sambaddhavṛṣaṇaḥ so 'pi hyajayā kūpalabdhayā /
kālaṃ bahutithaṃ bhadre kāmairnādyāpi tuṣyati
11. tathāhaṃ kṛpaṇaḥ subhru bhavatyāḥ premayantritaḥ /
ātmānaṃ nābhijānāmi mohitastava māyayā
12. yat pṛthivyāṃ vrīhiyavaṃ hiraṇyaṃ paśavaḥ striyaḥ /
na duhyanti manaḥprītiṃ puṃsaḥ kāmahatasya te
13. na jātu kāmaḥ kāmānāmupabhogena śāṃyati /
haviṣā kṛṣṇavartmeva bhūya evābhivardhate
14. yadā na kurute bhāvaṃ sarvabhūteṣv amaṅgalam /
samadṛṣṭestadā puṃsaḥ sarvāḥ sukhamayā diśaḥ
15. yā dustyajā durmatibhirjīryato yā na jīryate /
tāṃ tṛṣṇāṃ duḥkhanivahāṃ śarmakāmo drutaṃ tyajet
16. mātrā svasrā duhitrā vā nāviviktāsano bhavet /
balavān indriyagrāmo vidvāṃsamapi karṣati
17. pūrṇaṃ varṣasahasraṃ me viṣayān sevato 'sakṛt /
tathāpi cānusavanaṃ tṛṣṇā teṣūpajāyate
18. tasmādetāmahaṃ tyaktvā brahmaṇyadhyāya mānasam /
nirdvandvo nirahaṅkāraścariṣyāmi mṛgaiḥ saha
19. dṛṣṭaṃ śrutamasadbuddhvā nānudhyāyen na sandiśet /
saṃsṛtiṃ cātmanāśaṃ ca tatra vidvān sa ātmadṛk
20. ityuktvā nāhuṣo jāyāṃ tadīyaṃ pūrave vayaḥ /
dattvā svajarasaṃ tasmādādade vigataspṛhaḥ
21. diśi dakṣiṇapūrvasyāṃ druhyuṃ dakṣiṇato yadum /
pratīcyāṃ turvasuṃ cakra udīcyāmanumīśvaram
22. bhūmaṇḍalasya sarvasya pūrumarhattamaṃ viśām /
abhiṣicyāgrajāṃstasya vaśe sthāpya vanaṃ yayau
23. āsevitaṃ varṣapūgān ṣaḍvargaṃ viṣayeṣu saḥ /
kṣaṇena mumuce nīḍaṃ jātapakṣa iva dvijaḥ
24. sa tatra nirmuktasamastasaṅga ātmānubhūtyā vidhutatriliṅgaḥ /
pare 'male brahmaṇi vāsudeve lebhe gatiṃ bhāgavatīṃ pratītaḥ
25. śrutvā gāthāṃ devayānī mene prastobhamātmanaḥ /
strīpuṃsoḥ snehavaiklavyāt parihāsamiveritam
26. sā sannivāsaṃ suhṛdāṃ prapāyāmiva gacchatām /
vijñāyeśvaratantrāṇāṃ māyāviracitaṃ prabhoḥ
27. sarvatra saṅgamutsṛjya svapnaupamyena bhārgavī /
kṛṣṇe manaḥ samāveśya vyadhunol liṅgamātmanaḥ
28. namastubhyaṃ bhagavate vāsudevāya vedhase /
sarvabhūtādhivāsāya śāntāya bṛhate namaḥ
