3. kiṃ va uccaritairmāturdhāvadbhiḥ pṛṣṭhato hataiḥ /

4. yadi vaḥ pradhane śraddhā sāraṃ vā kṣullakā hṛdi /

5. evaṃ suragaṇān kruddho bhīṣayan vapuṣā ripūn /

nipeturmūrcchitā bhūmau yathaivāśaninā hatāḥ
7. mamarda padbhyāṃ surasainyamāturaṃ nimīlitākṣaṃ raṇaraṅgadurmadaḥ /

8. vilokya taṃ vajradharo 'tyamarṣitaḥ svaśatrave 'bhidravate mahāgadām /

9. sa indraśatruḥ kupito bhṛśaṃ tayā mahendravāhaṃ gadayoruvikramaḥ /
jaghāna kumbhasthala unnadan mṛdhe tat karma sarve samapūjayan nṛpa
10. airāvato vṛtragadābhimṛṣṭo vighūrṇito 'driḥ kuliśāhato yathā /
