1. sanniyacchābhibho manyuṃ nibodha śrāvitaṃ ca me /
sarvataḥ sāram ādatte yathā madhu-karo budhaḥ
2. asmin loke 'thavāmuṣmin munibhis tattva-darśibhiḥ /
dṛṣṭā yogāḥ prayuktāś ca puṃsāṃ śreyaḥ-prasiddhaye
3. tān ātiṣṭhati yaḥ samyag upāyān pūrva-darśitān /
avaraḥ śraddhayopeta upeyān vindate 'ñjasā
4. tān anādṛtya yo 'vidvān arthān ārabhate svayam /
tasya vyabhicaranty arthā ārabdhāś ca punaḥ punaḥ
5. purā sṛṣṭā hy oṣadhayo brahmaṇā yā viśāmpate /
bhujyamānā mayā dṛṣṭā asadbhir adhṛta-vrataiḥ
6. apālitānādṛtā ca bhavadbhir loka-pālakaiḥ /
corī-bhūte 'tha loke 'haṃ yajñārthe 'grasam oṣadhīḥ
7. nūnaṃ tā vīrudhaḥ kṣīṇā mayi kālena bhūyasā /
tatra yogena dṛṣṭena bhavān ādātum arhati
8. vatsaṃ kalpaya me vīra yenāhaṃ vatsalā tava /
dhokṣye kṣīramayān kāmān anurūpaṃ ca dohanam
9. dogdhāraṃ ca mahā-bāho bhūtānāṃ bhūta-bhāvana /
annam īpsitam ūrjasvad bhagavān vāñchate yadi
10. samāṃ ca kuru māṃ rājan deva-vṛṣṭaṃ yathā payaḥ /
apartāv api bhadraṃ te upāvarteta me vibho
11. iti priyaṃ hitaṃ vākyaṃ bhuva ādāya bhūpatiḥ /
vatsaṃ kṛtvā manuṃ pāṇāv aduhat sakalauṣadhīḥ
12. tathāpare ca sarvatra sāram ādadate budhāḥ /
tato 'nye ca yathā-kāmaṃ duduhuḥ pṛthu-bhāvitām
13. ṛṣayo duduhur devīm indriyeṣv atha sattama /
vatsaṃ bṛhaspatiṃ kṛtvā payaś chandomayaṃ śuci
14. kṛtvā vatsaṃ sura-gaṇā indraṃ somam adūduhan /
hiraṇmayena pātreṇa vīryam ojo balaṃ payaḥ
15. daiteyā dānavā vatsaṃ prahlādam asurarṣabham /
vidhāyādūduhan kṣīram ayaḥ-pātre surāsavam
16. gandharvāpsaraso 'dhukṣan pātre padmamaye payaḥ /
vatsaṃ viśvāvasuṃ kṛtvā gāndharvaṃ madhu saubhagam
17. vatsena pitaro 'ryamṇā kavyaṃ kṣīram adhukṣata /
āma-pātre mahā-bhāgāḥ śraddhayā śrāddha-devatāḥ
18. prakalpya vatsaṃ kapilaṃ siddhāḥ saṅkalpanāmayīm /
siddhiṃ nabhasi vidyāṃ ca ye ca vidyādharādayaḥ
19. anye ca māyino māyām antardhānādbhutātmanām /
mayaṃ prakalpya vatsaṃ te duduhur dhāraṇāmayīm
20. yakṣa-rakṣāṃsi bhūtāni piśācāḥ piśitāśanāḥ /
bhūteśa-vatsā duduhuḥ kapāle kṣatajāsavam
21. tathāhayo dandaśūkāḥ sarpā nāgāś ca takṣakam /
vidhāya vatsaṃ duduhur bila-pātre viṣaṃ payaḥ
22. paśavo yavasaṃ kṣīraṃ vatsaṃ kṛtvā ca go-vṛṣam /
araṇya-pātre cādhukṣan mṛgendreṇa ca daṃṣṭriṇaḥ
23. kravyādāḥ prāṇinaḥ kravyaṃ duduhuḥ sve kalevare /
suparṇa-vatsā vihagāś caraṃ cācaram eva ca
24. vaṭa-vatsā vanaspatayaḥ pṛthag rasamayaṃ payaḥ /
girayo himavad-vatsā nānā-dhātūn sva-sānuṣu
25. sarve sva-mukhya-vatsena sve sve pātre pṛthak payaḥ /
sarva-kāma-dughāṃ pṛthvīṃ duduhuḥ pṛthu-bhāvitām
26. evaṃ pṛthv-ādayaḥ pṛthvīm annādāḥ svannam ātmanaḥ /
doha-vatsādi-bhedena kṣīra-bhedaṃ kurūdvaha
27. tato mahīpatiḥ prītaḥ sarva-kāma-dughāṃ pṛthuḥ /
duhitṛtve cakāremāṃ premṇā duhitṛ-vatsalaḥ
28. cūrṇayan sva-dhanuṣ-koṭyā giri-kūṭāni rāja-rāṭ /
bhū-maṇḍalam idaṃ vainyaḥ prāyaś cakre samaṃ vibhuḥ
29. athāsmin bhagavān vainyaḥ prajānāṃ vṛttidaḥ pitā /
nivāsān kalpayāṃ cakre tatra tatra yathārhataḥ
30. grāmān puraḥ pattanāni durgāṇi vividhāni ca /
ghoṣān vrajān sa-śibirān ākarān kheṭa-kharvaṭān
31. prāk pṛthor iha naivaiṣā pura-grāmādi-kalpanā /
yathā-sukhaṃ vasanti sma tatra tatrākutobhayāḥ
