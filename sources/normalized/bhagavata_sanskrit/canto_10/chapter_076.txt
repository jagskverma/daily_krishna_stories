1. śiśupāla-sakhaḥ śālvo rukmiṇy-udvāha āgataḥ /
yadubhir nirjitaḥ saṅkhye jarāsandhādayas tathā
2. śālvaḥ pratijñām akaroc chṛṇvatāṃ sarva-bhūbhujām /
ayādavāṃ kṣmāṃ kariṣye pauruṣaṃ mama paśyata
3. iti mūḍhaḥ pratijñāya devaṃ paśu-patiṃ prabhum /
ārādhayām āsa nṛpaḥ pāṃśu-muṣṭiṃ sakṛd grasan
4. saṃvatsarānte bhagavān āśu-toṣa umā-patiḥ /
vareṇa cchandayām āsa śālvaṃ śaraṇam āgatam
5. devāsura-manuṣyāṇāṃ gandharvoraga-rakṣasām /
abhedyaṃ kāma-gaṃ vavre sa yānaṃ vṛṣṇi-bhīṣaṇam
6. tatheti giriśādiṣṭo mayaḥ para-puraṃ-jayaḥ /
puraṃ nirmāya śālvāya prādāt saubham ayas-mayam
7. sa labdhvā kāma-gaṃ yānaṃ tamo-dhāma durāsadam /
yayas dvāravatīṃ śālvo vairaṃ vṛṣṇi-kṛtaṃ smaran
8. nirudhya senayā śālvo mahatyā bharatarṣabha /
purīṃ babhañjopavanān udyānāni ca sarvaśaḥ
9. sa-gopurāṇi dvārāṇi prāsādāṭṭāla-tolikāḥ /
vihārān sa vimānāgryān nipetuḥ śastra-vṛṣṭayaḥ
10. śilā-drumāś cāśanayaḥ sarpā āsāra-śarkarāḥ /
pracaṇḍaś cakravāto 'bhūd rajasācchāditā diśaḥ
11. ity ardyamānā saubhena kṛṣṇasya nagarī bhṛśam /
nābhyapadyata śaṃ rājaṃs tri-pureṇa yathā mahī
12. pradyumno bhagavān vīkṣya bādhyamānā nijāḥ prajāḥ /
ma bhaiṣṭety abhyadhād vīro rathārūḍho mahā-yaśāḥ
13. sātyakiś cārudeṣṇaś ca sāmbo 'krūraḥ sahānujaḥ /
hārdikyo bhānuvindaś ca gadaś ca śuka-sāraṇau
14. apare ca maheṣv-āsā ratha-yūthapa-yūthapāḥ /
niryayur daṃśitā guptā rathebhāśva-padātibhiḥ
15. tataḥ pravavṛte yuddhaṃ śālvānāṃ yadubhiḥ saha /
yathāsurāṇāṃ vibudhais tumulaṃ loma-harṣaṇam
16. tāś ca saubha-pater māyā divyāstrai rukmiṇī-sutaḥ /
kṣaṇena nāśayām āsa naiśaṃ tama ivoṣṇa-guḥ
17. vivyādha pañca-viṃśatyā svarṇa-puṅkhair ayo-mukhaiḥ /
śālvasya dhvajinī-pālaṃ śaraiḥ sannata-parvabhiḥ
18. śatenātāḍayac chālvam ekaikenāsya sainikān /
daśabhir daśabhir netṝn vāhanāni tribhis tribhiḥ
19. tad adbhutaṃ mahat karma pradyumnasya mahātmanaḥ /
dṛṣṭvā taṃ pūjayām āsuḥ sarve sva-para-sainikāḥ
20. bahu-rūpaika-rūpaṃ tad dṛśyate na ca dṛśyate /
māyā-mayaṃ maya-kṛtaṃ durvibhāvyaṃ parair abhūt
21. kvacid bhūmau kvacid vyomni giri-mūrdhni jale kvacit /
alāta-cakra-vad bhrāmyat saubhaṃ tad duravasthitam
22. yatra yatropalakṣyeta sa-saubhaḥ saha-sainikaḥ /
śālvas tatas tato 'muñcañ charān sātvata-yūthapāḥ
23. śarair agny-arka-saṃsparśair āśī-viṣa-durāsadaiḥ /
pīḍyamāna-purānīkaḥ śālvo 'muhyat pareritaiḥ
24. śālvānīkapa-śastraughair vṛṣṇi-vīrā bhṛśārditāḥ /
na tatyajū raṇaṃ svaṃ svaṃ loka-dvaya-jigīṣavaḥ
25. śālvāmātyo dyumān nāma pradyumnaṃ prak prapīḍitaḥ /
āsādya gadayā maurvyā vyāhatya vyanadad balī
26. pradyumnaṃ gadayā sīrṇa- vakṣaḥ-sthalam ariṃ-damam /
apovāha raṇāt sūto dharma-vid dārukātmajaḥ
27. labdha-samjño muhūrtena kārṣṇiḥ sārathim abravīt /
aho asādhv idaṃ sūta yad raṇān me 'pasarpaṇam
28. na yadūnāṃ kule jātaḥ śrūyate raṇa-vicyutaḥ /
vinā mat klība-cittena sūtena prāpta-kilbiṣāt
29. kiṃ nu vakṣye 'bhisaṅgamya pitarau rāma-keśavau /
yuddhāt samyag apakrāntaḥ pṛṣṭas tatrātmanaḥ kṣamam
30. vyaktaṃ me kathayiṣyanti hasantyo bhrātṛ-jāmayaḥ /
klaibyaṃ kathaṃ kathaṃ vīra tavānyaiḥ kathyatāṃ mṛdhe

sūtaḥ kṛcchra-gataṃ rakṣed rathinaṃ sārathiṃ rathī
32. etad viditvā tu bhavān mayāpovāhito raṇāt /
upasṛṣṭaḥ pareṇeti mūrcchito gadayā hataḥ
